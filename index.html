A letter for you

Something I want you to remember, Baby.

My dearest Melat, ❤️

Happy first anniversary, Baby. I honestly don't know where to begin. One year sounds like just a number, but to me, it carries countless conversations, laughter, teasing, little arguments, silly moments, and feelings that words sometimes struggle to explain.

You know what's funny? You can be so naughty, so stubborn, and such a professional troublemaker sometimes. 😂 Yet somehow, you're also the person whose happiness matters so much to me. You're smart, caring, lovely, and beautifully yourself. I wouldn't trade that combination for anything.

My Sweetie, you have become such a special part of my everyday life. Sometimes a simple conversation with you can change my entire mood. Sometimes I smile at my phone like an idiot. And sometimes I just wish I could skip the distance between us and be beside you.

You're my sweet painkiller. Not because life never hurts, but because your presence can make even a difficult day feel lighter. I hope you always know how much your existence means to me.

We haven't lived every dream we've talked about yet. There are still hugs waiting, moments waiting, adventures waiting, and memories we haven't made. But I look forward to those days.

I don't promise that every day will be perfect. I promise to keep learning, listening, caring, laughing with you, and choosing us through the imperfect days too.

Thank you for this first chapter, Melat. Thank you for being my Baby, my Sweetie, my favorite headache, and my sweet painkiller.

If I could go back to that night, November 1, 2025, at 11:00 PM, knowing everything I know today... I'd still choose to begin this story with you.

Happy first anniversary, my love. This is only our first chapter. ❤️

Forever your favorite troublemaker. ❤️
